Heyy Faheem… ❤️

Mere dil ki ek aisi baat, jo main aaj tak tumse keh nahi paayi…

Faheem, Pata nahi main apni baat kahan se shuru karun, kyunki tumhare baare mein likhne baithti hoon toh alfaaz kam padne lagte hain. Tum mere liye sirf ek insaan nahi ho, balki meri zindagi ka woh hissa ho jise maine saalon se apne dil mein sambhaal kar rakha hai. Mujhe aaj bhi 7th standard ke woh din yaad hain, jab maine tumhe pehli baar us nazar se dekhna shuru kiya tha. Shayad us waqt mujhe khud bhi nahi pata tha ki ek chhoti si pasand mere dil mein itni gehri jagah bana legi. School ke din beet gaye, hum bade ho gaye, zindagi aage badhti gayi, lekin tumhare liye mere dil mein jo jagah thi, woh aaj bhi wahi hai. Tumhe shayad kabhi ehsaas bhi nahi hua hoga ki school mein koi tumhe itni khamoshi se dekhta tha. Jab tum volleyball khelte the, dining hall mein khana khaate the ya school mein apni duniya mein busy rehte the, main tumhe dekha karti thi. Tumhari chhoti-chhoti baatein bhi mujhe yaad reh jaati thi. Mere liye woh aam pal the hi nahi, kyunki unmein tum the. Mujhe tumhari padhai par bhi bahut garv hai. Tumne 10th board mein 98% haasil kiye aur aaj apne sapnon ki taraf badh rahe ho. Tumhari kamyabi dekhkar mujhe khushi hoti hai. Main dil se dua karti hoon ki Allah tumhe zindagi mein aur bhi kamyabi de, tumhare har achhe sapne ko poora kare aur tumhe hamesha khush rakhe. Faheem, tumhe pata hai, mere paas tumhara number school ke time se tha, lekin kabhi tumhe message ya call karne ki himmat nahi hui. Main har saal tumhare birthday ka intezaar karti thi, magar wish karne ki himmat nahi juta paati thi. Phir 14 September 2026 ko, apne birthday ke aas-paas, maine khud ko himmat di aur tumhe message kiya. Jab tumhara message aaya, toh mere liye woh ek chhoti si baat nahi thi. Shayad tumhare liye woh ek normal conversation thi, lekin mere liye usmein saalon ki khamoshi aur bahut saari feelings chhupi hui thi. Kabhi-kabhi mujhe lagta hai ki shayad tum mujhe bhool gaye ho. Shayad tumhe school ki woh ladki yaad bhi na ho jo tumhe chupke se dekha karti thi. Lekin main tumhe kaise samjhaun ki kuch log hamari zindagi mein roz saamne na hote hue bhi hamare dil aur yaadon mein hamesha rehte hain. Main tumse yeh nahi kehti ki tum bhi mujhe utna hi chaho jitna main tumhe chahti hoon. Mohabbat koi aisi cheez nahi jo kisi se maangi ja sake. Bas kabhi-kabhi dil chahta hai ki tum bhi mujhe ek baar us nazar se dekho, jis nazar se main tumhe dekhti aayi hoon. Ek baar tum bhi mujhe us nazar se dekhna, jis nazar se main tumhe dekhti hoon. Shayad tab tum samajh paoge ki meri khamoshi ke peeche kitni saari baatein chhupi hui hain. Mujhe nahi pata ki hamari zindagi mein kabhi milna likha hai ya nahi. Lekin agar kabhi Allah ne humein milaya, toh main us pal ko apni zindagi ki ek khoobsurat yaad bana kar rakhungi. Main tumse sirf ek baar milna chahti hoon, tumse baat karna chahti hoon aur tumhe saamne se dekhkar yeh mehsoos karna chahti hoon ki jis insaan ko maine itne saalon se apni yaadon mein rakha, woh aaj mere saamne hai. Tumhari zindagi mein bahut saare log aayenge, tum bahut aage badhoge aur tumhari duniya mein bahut kuch badlega. Ho sakta hai main tumhari zindagi ka kabhi ek chhota sa hissa bhi na ban paun. Lekin meri taraf se tumhare liye hamesha achhi duaayein rahengi. Main chahti hoon ki tum apne sapne poore karo, ek achhe insaan bano aur zindagi mein hamesha khush raho. Tumhari khushi mere liye important hai, chahe us khushi mein meri koi jagah ho ya na ho. Aur aakhir mein bas itna kehna chahti hoon… Tum meri zindagi ki woh kahani ho, jiska anjaam mujhe nahi pata, lekin jise maine apne dil mein bahut mohabbat se sambhaal kar rakha hai. Agar tumhare dil mein mere liye kabhi thodi si bhi jagah bane, toh mujhe jaanne ka ek mauka zaroor dena. Aur agar tumhari feelings alag hon, toh main tumhari sachchai ki bhi izzat karungi. Kyunki tumhe apni feelings batana mera haq hai, lekin tumhari feelings ka faisla karna tumhara haq hai. Allah tumhe hamesha apni hifazat mein rakhe, Faheem. Tumhari zindagi khushiyon, kamyabi aur sukoon se bhari rahe. Tumhari woh school wali ladki, Nasreen ❤️
